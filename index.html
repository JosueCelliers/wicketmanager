How a career works

Make your manager

Name, nationality, age, background — ex-player, coach, analyst or businessman, each with a perk. Your country's side and leagues come calling more often.

Collect jobs, don't just switch them

Offers come from your reputation. Hold a club, a T20 franchise and a national side at the same time, each in its own window of the year. Sacked from one? You keep the rest.

Play until they retire you

Auctions every year, bilateral series, a T20 World Championship every two years and a 50-over one every four, world rankings, your club players called up once you run the national side too. Retire at 70 with a legacy ceremony.
